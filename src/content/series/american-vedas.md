figures from south asian religious iconography, rendered on bare white grounds.

stripped of their ornament and color, they lose the marks that once made them holy — and nearly the marks that made them recognizable.

what remains is flat, simplified, faintly undignified: the visual language of the information era, turned on the sacred.
